Who we are

SVG Studio is an independent design studio in Amsterdam, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 98752286. For anything about your data, email svg.graphix@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email, business, current website, the services you tick, budget, timing and your message. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking cookies on this site.

Why

To answer your inquiry and, if you want, prepare a proposal. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible agreement (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). We never sell your details or add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the form to our inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts that inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Fonts load from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr and Cloudflare, which means those services see your IP address when the page loads.

Some of these companies are based outside the EU and work under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that don't become a project are deleted within 12 months. If we work together, project emails are kept for the duration of the project, and invoices for 7 years as Dutch tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Stored in your browser

The site remembers your language choice (local storage) and whether you've seen the intro this visit (session storage). Neither leaves your device.
